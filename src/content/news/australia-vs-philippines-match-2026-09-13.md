The [Australian Go Association](https://australiango.asn.au/news/australian-vs-philippines-team-challenge/) has announced the opening pairing for the **Australia vs Philippines Match**.

**Sunday, 13 September 2026 at 12:00 PM** — **Leo Chen 4k** (Australia) vs **Vince Gerald Azarcon 5k** (Philippines).

This is the first game of a five-week team challenge: one game a week, with the winner progressing. Later weekends are **19–20 September**, **26–27 September**, **3–4 October**, and **10–11 October**.

Cheer on Vince and the rest of the Philippine side as they take on Australia.

Full details and later pairings: [Australia vs Philippines Team Challenge](/events/australia-vs-philippines-team-challenge-2026/).
