The Go Federation of the Philippines (**GoPinas**) and **KimonoPh** are coming together for a special **Igo Meet-Up & Cooking Party**, featuring **Kinya**, a 5-dan Japanese Igo player.

**Sunday, 26 July 2026** at **Cucina Kin-chan**, [Tower 2, The St. Francis Shangri-La Place](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tower+2,+The+St+Francis+shangrila+Place/@14.5798471,121.0563872,17z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x3397c90078fcd4b7:0x2358a071edf8d5c2!8m2!3d14.5798471!4d121.0563872!16s%2Fg%2F11z7mhlswc) (TSFSP), Ortigas Center, Mandaluyong.

- **5:00 PM – 8:00 PM** — Dinner Party ([Add to Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE&text=Dinner%20Party&dates=20260726T170000/20260726T200000&ctz=Asia/Manila&location=Cucina%20Kin-chan%2C%20Tower%202%2C%20The%20St.%20Francis%20Shangri-La%20Place%20%28TSFSP%29%2C%20Ortigas%20Center%2C%20Mandaluyong&details=Igo%20Meet-Up%20%26%20Cooking%20Party%20dinner%20at%20Cucina%20Kin-chan%20with%20GoPinas%20and%20KimonoPh.%20Maps%3A%20https%3A%2F%2Fmaps.app.goo.gl%2FxActz4sq9M8DmyjZA))

Looking forward to an afternoon of Go, friendship, cultural exchange, and good food!
