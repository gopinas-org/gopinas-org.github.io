The **Go Federation of the Philippines** is playing an online team challenge against the **Australian Go Association**: one game a week for five weeks, with the winner progressing.

**First game — Sunday, 13 September 2026 at 12:00 PM**

- **Leo Chen 4k** (Australia) vs **Vince Gerald Azarcon 5k** (Philippines)

Later weekends: **19–20 September**, **26–27 September**, **3–4 October**, and **10–11 October**.

The series is online. Come cheer on Vince and the Philippine side.
