The Go Federation of the Philippines (**GoPinas**) and **KimonoPh** are coming together for a special **Igo Meet-Up & Cooking Party**, featuring **Kinya**, a 5-dan Japanese Igo player.

**Sunday, 26 July 2026** at **[Cucina Kin-chan](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cucina%20Kin-chan%20The%20St.%20Francis%20Shangri-La%20Place%20Tower%202%20Mandaluyong)**, The St. Francis Shangri-La Place (TSFSP), Tower 2.

- **2:00 PM – 5:00 PM** — Igo Meet-Up ([Add to Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE&text=Igo%20Meet-Up&dates=20260726T140000/20260726T170000&ctz=Asia/Manila&location=Cucina%20Kin-chan%2C%20The%20St.%20Francis%20Shangri-La%20Place%20%28TSFSP%29%2C%20Tower%202%2C%20Ortigas%20Center%2C%20Mandaluyong&details=GoPinas%20and%20KimonoPh%20Igo%20Meet-Up%20featuring%20Kinya%2C%20a%205-dan%20Japanese%20Igo%20player.))
- **5:00 PM – 8:00 PM** — Dinner Party ([Add to Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE&text=Dinner%20Party&dates=20260726T170000/20260726T200000&ctz=Asia/Manila&location=Cucina%20Kin-chan%2C%20The%20St.%20Francis%20Shangri-La%20Place%20%28TSFSP%29%2C%20Tower%202%2C%20Ortigas%20Center%2C%20Mandaluyong&details=Igo%20Meet-Up%20%26%20Cooking%20Party%20dinner%20at%20Cucina%20Kin-chan%20with%20GoPinas%20and%20KimonoPh.))
